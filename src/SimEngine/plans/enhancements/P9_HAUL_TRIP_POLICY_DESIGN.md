## 0. Problem

LogisticsService.Preview selects a structurally feasible carrier without checking whether
its current operating inventory can fund the complete selected movement. Existing stock
bands/outlet reserves own retention, but no optional authored trip rule applies that budget.
Service interval orchestration is also still missing; it must not be replaced by instant repair.

## 1. Goals

1. Optional master-listed haul_trip_profiles and haul_trip_policy declare trip requirements.
2. Fresh selected admission uses existing OperatingInputsContracts and resource transaction
   evaluation, not another consumption/fuel/rounding/stock/claim authority.
3. Existing outlet reserve floors may be preserved after movement; their amounts are never copied
   into trip settings. Replenishment desired stock remains distinct from a burn reserve floor.
4. Return-cargo commitments cannot be borrowed as operating inputs.
5. Normal commands, private matching previews, provider contracts and one embedded Sync own policy.
6. Retained-job replanning and later physical service intervals use existing jobs and GameClock.

## 2. Design

B11.3a adds IHaulTripPolicy/Definition and IHaulTripProfile through the existing LogisticsModule.
HaulTripSettings holds profile identity, enabled and preserve_outlet_reserve only. Disabled
means hauling disabled for that carrier; absence means no authored trip requirement. Native
content requires explicit booleans; missing profile/capabilities fail before the first Update.
Work/load/home/area/stock policies remain unchanged and are not mirrored.

HaulTripPolicyContracts reads the selected MotionTravelEstimate, delegates the movement bill to
OperatingInputsContracts.Read and evaluates its combined bill through IResourceTransaction.
Preserved floors come from the actual operating inventory's IOutletPolicy. Emergency-only reserve
access is honored by that existing setting, but emergency never bypasses physical funding.
Returning cargo sharing that inventory adds its unclaimed return amount to the required bill;
future incoming shipments cannot fund the approach or be borrowed while protected by claims.
The check creates no promise or state. Custom authored profile checks may add refusals, not bypass
the engine's mandatory funding/shape/ownership rules. Provider reads remain observation-guarded.

LogisticsService.Preview invokes this after the actual source/destination selection and native
load size, before claims/jobs/interruption. Ready HaulPreview carries the detached selected budget;
refused candidates publish only the typed blocker. Acquire's existing fresh preview repeats it.
ConfigureHaulTripPolicyCommand and private management previews share the same admission.
One capability's Sync stores settings; no fuel ledger, route model, timer or policy table.
