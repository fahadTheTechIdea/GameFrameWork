OilCo.Game owns oil-and-gas gameplay through public framework extension contracts. Oil clients only
simulate players or present engine results. All domain decisions still run inside engine assemblies,
not in a harness, headless executable or demo.

## Implementation sequence

1. Compose a required oil module with declared framework capabilities. Move no geology/reservoir model,
   well physics, oil company rules, oil AI playbooks or campaign thresholds into framework core.
   Optional oil storyteller/fog modules depend explicitly on oil capabilities, not implicit installation.
2. Keep the oil master JSON manifest as the game composition root. Import framework standard content
   once through declared sources; author oil resources, vehicles, structures, technology, activities,
   operation states, research effects, hazards, objective predicates, scenarios and playbooks in domain
   JSON. Remove replaced duplicate generic definitions and old schema fields instead of tolerating both.
3. Migrate oil research/campaign/pacing callers to the promoted mechanisms with oil-specific effect and
   predicate providers. Preserve scientific/gameplay formulas in oil; do not relocate them into clients.
4. Author the drilling DAG as material supply → site preparation → drilling → casing/cementing →
   completion, using actual provider roles, tools, fluids, duration and effects. Seismic and logging are
   optional knowledge branches. Validate and test that a legal unsurveyed site can be prepared, drilled
   and completed with sufficient resources; lack of knowledge can affect risk, not invent a prerequisite.
5. Migrate construction, production, survey, recovery, repair and network-laying workflows through the
   same graph/work owners. Preserve physical machines moving, loading, unloading, constructing,
   repairing and returning over game time; no immediate command-completion substitute.
6. Keep incident propagation, oil environmental accounting, preparedness, unsafe-operation isolation and
   restart conditions in oil handlers using framework incident/response/control contracts. Respect ordinary
   resource, work and route constraints during emergencies. No combat or population feature is added.
7. Keep rivals as ordinary companies using public seat views, previews and commands, with no privileged
   resource grants, entity access, instant placement or free recovery. Private surveys/subsurface data and
   undiscovered activity remain scoped by the visibility/knowledge provider.
8. Audit every headless/harness/demo conditional, not only forbidden namespaces. Distinguish a player's
   strategic choice from engine facts: choosing to research/build is legitimate; calculating validity,
   cost, route, completion, work priority, production state or hazard recovery is not.
9. Replace `FieldPlayer.GeneratorRunning`'s fuel-stock inference with an engine-reported operation fact.
   Search for similar stock/state guesses, placement arithmetic, deadlines, recipe economics and
   locally maintained work progress. Add missing engine views rather than reproduce formulas in clients.
10. Reconcile oil design documents and original revision tracker. Extraction status lives only in the new
    master. Oil-domain activity/industrial progress remains in its existing authoritative tracker; retire
    obsolete generic executor designs rather than maintain two live plans for the same responsibility.

## Host boundary examples

Allowed: submit a player command, choose a target from engine previews, request an Update, pause/speed
through approved clock controls, read notifications/snapshots, and display results.

Not allowed: decide a well is complete from elapsed frames, transfer cargo locally, infer a processor is
operating from inventory, select a valid road path with client arithmetic, change authoritative entity
state, grant rival materials, implement project requirements, or run a local hazard simulator.

Draw/rendering is deferred to a future game platform, not part of SimEngine or this migration.
A scripted player may choose strategy, but that strategy must use the
same command refusal semantics and engine-calculated facts available to a human player.
