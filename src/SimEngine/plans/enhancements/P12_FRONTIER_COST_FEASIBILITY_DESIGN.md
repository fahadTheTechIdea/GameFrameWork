same optional account independently. Reuse the existing WorkBoard cost admission and existing

money remains absent, not a fake ledger. Every prospective read stays private, immutable and pure.

## Design and single ownership

WorkStart.cs.CheckCostSet widens to aligned actual requirement results/contexts. Its single-node
wrapper delegates to that same evaluator. Aggregate existing unspent held receipts before SAME
MonetaryBudgetExecution.CheckSpend; aggregate unbound ordinary amounts before SAME CanPay.
Spent obligations and explicitly credit-authorized/disabled costs do not demand a second payment.
Use wide transient arithmetic and typed admission refusal for an unrepresentable ordinary total,
not overflow or a fabricated clamped amount. Validate actual same-session/company contexts.
No new ledger, reservation, money policy, budget provider, paid state or selector.

WorkIncidentPreparedness.cs exposes nullable FrontierCostBlocker: null means missing individual
criteria/coverage, None means the current combined obligation fits. ReadyForFrontier also requires
it. Existing actual cost facts stay on each row; no ambiguous cross-node copied occurrence store.
Actual jobs still recheck and reserve through the original work/account owners. Readiness is not
atomic team acquisition or future funding authorization. Resource and dock checks stay their owners.
