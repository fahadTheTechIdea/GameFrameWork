- Four-minute stabilization consumes two volume units of suppressant and worker fuel. Twelve-minute repair consumes two spare units and fuel, holding one reusable tool. - Minute/half-minute variants use the same authored durations, graphs and quantities, independent sessions and one clock per session.

The site has explicit compatible off-corridor crane/land bays and a road bay; the depot has an off-corridor waiting slot.
