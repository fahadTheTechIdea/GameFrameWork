# Lost WWII soldier returning to base / completing a mission: precedent screen

Date: 24 October 2026. Status: screened; not distinct at the premise level; retained as research history.

## Owner proposal screened

A WWII soldier, or possibly a small unit, becomes separated from friendly forces and must reach base again or complete a mission while isolated behind enemy lines. The intended experience could include navigation, scarcity, combat, stealth and mission choice.

## Direct and close precedents

- [Broken Lines](https://brokenlinesgame.com/) is a WWII story-driven tactical RPG about eight soldiers stranded behind enemy lines. Its stated goal is to guide them through missions and back home, with real-time execution, morale, desertion risk and character drama. This is the closest direct whole-game precedent for the squad interpretation.
- [OUTGUNNED - Behind Enemy Lines](https://www.dicesterstudio.com/outgunned/) describes a single-player WWII-vibe action-adventure in which “The Lost Soldier” is trapped behind enemy territory, navigates terrain, evades patrols and fights to escape. This is a direct precedent for the lone-soldier interpretation, although its announced format is a platformer rather than an RTS.
- [Extraktion 1943](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2631080/Extraktion_1943/) describes a stranded Allied soldier behind enemy lines, with survival resources, sabotage, assassination, a hideout/resistance network and extraction. It is listed as coming soon, so this is a product-page/announcement precedent rather than verified shipped gameplay.
- [Partisans 1941](https://partisans-game.com/) places a Red Army commander behind enemy lines, gathering a squad, building a hidden camp, managing resources and undertaking sabotage missions. It is a released WWII real-time tactics precedent for the broader “isolated force survives and completes operations” structure.
- [Commandos: Behind Enemy Lines](https://store.steampowered.com/app/6800/Commandos_Behind_Enemy_Lines/) establishes the classic WWII real-time tactics loop of commanding a small squad behind enemy lines, completing hazardous missions and bringing the team back alive.
- [The Last Train Home](https://lasttrainhome.thqnordic.com/lasttrainhome/INT/win) is not WWII—it follows Czechoslovak Legion soldiers after World War I—but it demonstrates the adjacent RTS/survival structure of a separated force trying to get home while managing soldiers, supplies, morale and mission choices.

## Assessment

This is not distinct at the premise level. Both “one lost soldier trying to get back” and “a stranded WWII squad trying to survive, complete missions and return” already have direct precedents. Adding resource scarcity, stealth, a base, extraction, or branching mission objectives would refine the loop, but would not create a new whole-game identity by itself.

The closest exact collision is *Broken Lines* for the squad version and *OUTGUNNED* for the lone-soldier version. Do not revive this proposal merely by changing the camera, adding RTS controls, or renaming extraction as “return to base.”

No hands-on playtest was performed. The screen uses official developer, publisher and Steam descriptions; announcements are labelled as such, and absence of another match is not treated as proof of originality.
