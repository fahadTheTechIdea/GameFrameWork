# Whole-game identity screening

Date: 24 October 2026. Research only; no selected design, implementation, artwork or playtest.

## Corrected interpretation of the brief

Fahad explicitly clarified during this research that the Stardew Valley / soccer-management example was not a request to repeat that combination. It illustrated finding a surprising cross-genre identity. Daily life, a management layer, an embodied commander and relationships are therefore not selection requirements. Earlier living-company proposals were already rejected; do not bring them back under another name.

The prior traffic/ground-wear proposal was also rejected as a mechanic rather than a distinct game. Preserve its comparisons in NOVEL_CONCEPT_SCREENING.md as research history, not a shortlisted direction.

The research target is a different overall play experience that retains real-time strategic command: a distinctive player objective, repeatable session structure, progression and source of tension. No proposed concept clears the brief merely because its donor genre has not appeared in an obvious WWII setting.

## Historical grounding of the rescue lead, retained without recommendation

The [RAF's Air Publication 3232](https://www.raf.mod.uk/what-we-do/our-history/air-historical-branch/second-world-war-thematic-studies/ap-3232-air-sea-rescue/) documents WWII air-sea rescue organisation, aircraft, marine craft and expansion. This establishes a historical role, not game originality or fun. A period board game titled The Game of Air-Sea Rescue also surfaced in secondary catalogues; detailed first-party rules were not verified. Therefore even the claim that WWII rescue is an unused game subject would be unsound.

## Outcome and evidence limits

No new game concept has cleared Fahad's originality/distinctiveness screen in this pass. These are rejections or withheld leads, not a menu of recommendations. The searches produced useful collisions; they did not establish an unoccupied market.

This pass consulted developer/publisher/store descriptions, creator-hosted prototypes, creator announcements and a mod repository. Announcements establish prior proposed designs, not complete implementation. No downloads, hands-on sessions, player tests, sales analysis or exhaustive mod catalogue audit occurred. Search failures and absent feature descriptions do not prove absence. Some broad queries returned mostly irrelevant material and were not used as evidence.

Further evaluation must keep the user's corrected scope: explore across genres freely; compare the complete proposed experience before presenting it; retain real-time command and open strategic choices; do not automatically add daily-life or management systems. Any later recommendation must name its closest competitors and explain the difference in the actual recurring decisions, rather than asserting unprecedented ingredients.
